## Themings

**Nether Wastes**
- A small chance for Netherrack to become Nether Quartz Ore

**Crimson Forest**
- Netherrack becomes Crimson Nylium
- Crimson Nylium may sprout Crimson Fungus or Crimson Roots
- Rare Shroomlights growing above the Nylium

**Warped Forest**
- Netherrack becomes Warped Nylium
- Warped Nylium may sprout Warped Fungus, Warped Roots, or Twisting Vines
- Rare Shroomlights growing above the Nylium

**Soul Sand Valley**
- Netherrack becomes Soul Sand, with a chance of Bone Block instead
- Magma Blocks become Soul Soil
- Soul Sand and Soul Soil may spontaneously ignite with Soul Fire

**Basalt Deltas**
- Netherrack becomes Basalt
- Magma Blocks become Blackstone, with a chance of Gilded Blackstone instead
- A rare Wither Skeleton Skull may be found resting atop the blackened terrain
